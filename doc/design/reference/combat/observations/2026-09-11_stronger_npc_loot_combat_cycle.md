### Published injury and Doctor rules

The official [Injury](http://wiki.neverlands.ru/wiki/Травма) and
[Doctor](http://wiki.neverlands.ru/wiki/Доктор) pages were separately read over
public HTTP. They describe light, medium, heavy and combat injuries; heavy
injury blocks movement, and combat injury also blocks Inventory. The guaranteed
combat-injury case lasts24 hours, with6 additional hours for a subsequent one;
this is not the duration rule for the observed ordinary light injury.

Doctor treatment requires the Healer perk, Doctor skill/Knowledge, the matching
license and bag, same-cell presence and a patient outside combat. A paid request
requires patient confirmation; a zero-price request does not. The first bag
requires Knowledge5 and Doctor5. The observed player's Knowledge1 does not
satisfy that bag requirement. Published self-treatment excludes combat injury
and evaluates Knowledge after injury penalties. No treatment, patient request,
purchase or injury reset was performed. These are published workflow inputs;
ordinary injury probability/duration/penalty and a live end-to-end medical
interaction still require evidence. Local Healer ownership and licenses do not
by themselves implement treatment or injury persistence.
